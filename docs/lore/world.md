# World

> **Türkçe özet.** AIONIA, Fragaria Interactive çatısı altında çok dünyalı, genişlemeye açık bir kurgu evrenidir; havası sevimli, derin ve epiktir. Human OS bir sağlık uygulamasıdır ve evrenin bu havası ürün yüzeyine taşınmaz (AIONIA §11). Kaynaklar Human OS'un evrende nerede durduğunu tanımlamaz; aşağıdaki çerçeve bu yüzden ÖNERİ'dir.

## What the sources define (CANON)

- **AIONIA** is an original, multi-world fictional universe under **Fragaria Interactive**, open to expansion; it is not only a game and not a single planet (AIONIA §1; canon L-001, L-002).
- Its atmosphere is **cute, profound and epic**: curiosity, discovery, warmth, character feeling, ancient civilisations and large-scale mystery can live together; neither sugary-childish only nor constantly dark (§1).
- Canon has layers: ANA, YEREL, OYUN, ALTERNATİF, TASLAK (§2). A world record holds geography, ecosystem, culture, history, technology or magic rules and links to products (§2).
- AIONIA characters, pixel art and lore are used **only in suitable projects**; a health app gets no forced mascot, gamification, pixel art or epic narrative (§11).
- **Human OS** is an independent application of the owner. Fragaria Interactive and AIONIA are not shown as brands in it (`docs/DESIGN_BRIEF.md`; canon L-009). Aion is the one element of the universe it shows, quietly and optionally (L-006).

## What the sources do not define

No source places Human OS in the universe: no world, region, institution, history or rules. AIONIA §2 says unknown information is a gap, not a fact to invent automatically.

## Frame for future canon (PROPOSAL, L-012)

If the owner wants Human OS to have an in-world place, a world record would fill these fields (AIONIA §2). Every field is empty until the owner approves content.

| Field | Content | Status |
|---|---|---|
| Place in AIONIA (world, region or institution) | — | gap |
| Geography and ecosystem | — | gap |
| Culture | — | gap |
| History | — | gap |
| Technology or magic rules | — | gap |
| Link to the product | Aion appears in the app as a still picture only (L-006) | CANON |

Two constraints any proposal must keep:

1. The setting stays in these documents. It never becomes screens, stories, quests or text in the product.
2. The setting never explains, judges or frames health data. A person's record is real life, never part of a fiction (the same reason Build Lab synthetic data never mixes with real profiles).

One idea, only as a starting point for the owner: Aion as a quiet keeper of a person's own record, who shows where things are and never reads them (L-013, PROPOSAL).
