# Glossary

> **Türkçe özet.** Lore'daki adlar ve terimler, her birinin kaynağıyla. "Shortevity" ve "Axolotl" teması lore değildir; karışmasın diye burada belirtilir.

| Term | Meaning | Lore? | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| **AIONIA** | The owner's original, multi-world fictional universe, open to expansion. Also the name of his design and quality standard. | Yes (ANA) | AIONIA standard, header, §1, §11 |
| **AIONIA standard** | "AIONIA — Evrensel Dijital Ürün, Tasarım ve Kalite Standartları", the owner's Google Doc (v1.7, 13 November 2026). Normative; the repository records how it applies in [docs/AIONIA_STANDARD.md](../AIONIA_STANDARD.md). | Rules, not story | AIONIA standard §0, §8 |
| **Aion** | A character of the universe; in Human OS a pink axolotl in a white lab coat shown as a still picture. See [characters/aion.md](characters/aion.md). | Yes | AIONIA §1; D-029 (A001) |
| **Infinity-leaf emblem** | The emblem on Aion's lab coat. | Yes | D-029 (A001) |
| **Fragaria Interactive** | The owner's main product and publishing brand. Not shown as a brand in Human OS. | Brand | AIONIA header, §11; `docs/DESIGN_BRIEF.md` |
| **Fragaria Games** | Not a required sub-brand (removed in AIONIA v1.7). | Retired name | AIONIA §22.10 |
| **Human OS** | Also Human Health OS, Longevity App. The owner's independent, offline-first personal health record app. Its place in the universe is not defined (PROPOSAL L-012). | Product | `docs/HUMAN_OS_OVERVIEW.md`; `CLAUDE.md` |
| **Kurt, Kuzu, Baykuş Ömer, Tavşan Emre** | Other existing AIONIA characters. None appears in Human OS. | Yes (ANA, existence only) | AIONIA §4, §22.9 |
| **ANA, YEREL, OYUN, ALTERNATİF, TASLAK** | AIONIA canon layers: universe-wide, local, game, alternative continuity, draft. Mapped to this project's status levels in [canon.md](canon.md). | Method | AIONIA §2 |
| **CANON, APPROVED DRAFT, PROPOSAL, RETIRED** | This project's canon status levels. | Method | [canon.md](canon.md); D-051 |
| **Shortevity** | Product term from v0.24 (alias "Shortgevity") for a Build Lab channel opposite Longevity. **Not lore**; listed so it is not mistaken for one. | No | D-005 |
| **Axolotl (palette)** | One of the app's named colour palettes (U031, from YKSMania). An appearance choice, **not lore**; it does not add Aion anywhere. | No | U031 in `project_state/FORGE_LOG.md` |
